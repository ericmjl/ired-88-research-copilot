## Key claims

- A wild-type imine reductase was engineered for the reductive amination step
  of the LSD1 inhibitor GSK2879552 manufacturing process, with concomitant
  substrate amine resolution.
- Three rounds of evolution gave a variant with a **>38,000-fold improvement
  over wild type**; kilogram quantities of the key intermediate were produced
  in 84% yield, 99.9% purity, >99.7% ee, with improved process mass intensity.
- Beneficial mutations from evolution round one were spatially clustered with
  scikit-learn DBSCAN (script shipped in that paper's supporting
  information); the work used Codexis CodeEvolver platform tooling.
