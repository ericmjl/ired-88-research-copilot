## Key claims

- Reports the stereoselectivity and crystal structure (PDB **5FWN**) of an
  imine reductase from *Amycolatopsis orientalis* (AoIRED), one of the
  structurally characterized IREDs commonly used as a template.
- Other IRED engineering studies use AoIRED (5FWN) as a molecular-replacement
  or homology-model reference; e.g. the Gilio et al. 2022 review shows an
  IR-46 model derived from AoIRED 5FWN.

## Relevance to the demo questions

- Q2: IRED-88's own crystal (7OG3) removes the need for a homology model, but
  5FWN is the classic reference structure for the family.
- Q3: if a question touches IRED-wide structural features (dimer interface,
  cofactor-binding geometry), AoIRED is the comparison point.
